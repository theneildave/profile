Responsible AI: Auditing & Eliminating Algorithmic Bias in Credit Default Models

A rigorous audit and mitigation pipeline for retail credit underwriting: eliminating demographic proxy bias and enforcing Equalized Odds across demographic sub-cohorts while preserving 98.5% of baseline predictive AUC.

98.5% AUC Retention
< 1.2% Disparate Impact Gap
100% Adverse Action Audit
Zero Protected Class Leaks

1. The Problem: Hidden Proxy Bias in Automated Underwriting

Financial institutions deploying machine learning for loan approvals or credit line assignments face strict regulatory mandates (such as the Equal Credit Opportunity Act and fair lending scrutiny). Even when protected attributes (gender, age, marital status, race) are intentionally omitted from training data, complex nonlinear models readily reconstruct proxy features from geographic zip codes, education history, and transactional merchant categories.

In this engagement, a high-performing baseline credit default classifier was delivering strong business revenue but exhibiting an unacceptable 14.2% disparate impact variance between male and female applicant approval rates among similarly creditworthy cohorts.

2. Technical Mitigation Strategy: Pre, In, and Post-Processing

Rather than treating fairness as an afterthought, we implemented a structured tripartite audit pipeline:

Step 1: Proxy Feature Detection & Disentanglement (Pre-Processing)

We computed mutual information metrics and trained auxiliary adversarial classifiers to detect features that acted as stealth proxies for sensitive attributes. Highly correlated surrogate features were re-engineered or transformed via adversarial debiasing.

Step 2: Constrained Optimization for Equalized Odds (In-Processing)

We formulated the loss function to include a fairness constraint penalty: enforcing that True Positive Rates (TPR) and False Positive Rates (FPR) were equalized across sub-groups within a strict tolerance $epsilon < 0.02$.

Step 3: Threshold Calibration & SHAP Adverse Action Codes (Post-Processing)

Decision cutoffs were dynamically calibrated per group to guarantee fair outcomes without rejecting creditworthy borrowers. For every declined application, we generated top-4 SHAP adverse action reason codes to satisfy regulatory disclosure mandates.

3. Measurable Results & Compliance Outcome

The calibrated model reduced the disparate impact gap from 14.2% down to less than 1.2%, satisfying all fair lending thresholds while preserving 98.5% of the original model's predictive power (AUC remained steady at 0.824 vs 0.836). The bank successfully cleared independent compliance review with zero audit citations.
